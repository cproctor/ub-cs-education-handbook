## LAI 600: Curriculum Integration and Assessment {#lai-600}

This course offers an overview of school programs with a general foundation of and orientation
toward perspectives on historical development, educational research, curriculum organization, and
current trends in the learning sciences that inform school curriculum. Additionally, it addresses
formal and informal assessment including standardized assessments, state assessments, and
classroom-based alternative assessments.

*Note: the catalog lists this course as variable credit (1-3), rather than the fixed 3 credits
assumed elsewhere in this handbook; please confirm before relying on this description. LAI 600 is
not currently part of the required coursework for any track.*
