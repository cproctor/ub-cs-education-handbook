## LAI 698: Instructional Strategies in Inclusive Classrooms {#lai-698}

This graduate-level course targets pre-service teachers pursuing adolescence certification. It
emphasizes contemporary instructional approaches and requires students to ground work in research
and theory. The curriculum encompasses classroom management, organization, safety, and diversity
as a teaching resource, alongside strategies for supporting English language learners and students
with special needs. Additional topics include lesson planning aligned with New York State
standards, assessment procedures, technology integration, and collaboration with school personnel
and families.

- Prerequisite: Admission to the Teacher Education Institute program and enrollment in an
  appropriate UB graduate degree program.

*Note: the catalog lists this course as variable credit (1-3), rather than the fixed 3 credits
assumed elsewhere in this handbook; please confirm before relying on this description.*
